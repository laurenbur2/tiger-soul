Burnout · Reset · Bufo (5-MeO-DMT)

When Rest Isn't Enough

Burnout doesn't arrive all at once. It's the slow cost of carrying too much for too long, until the things that used to light you up feel like just more weight. Rest helps, but rest alone rarely reaches the part of you that forgot how to feel alive.

You're allowed to stop

What Burnout Really Is

More than tired

Burnout isn't only exhaustion. It's a kind of numbness, a flatness that pushing harder only deepens. You can sleep for a week and still feel hollow.

It's the body and spirit asking for something a vacation can't give: a real pause, a witness, and permission to finally put the weight down.

Medicine of the Gods

A Different Kind of Rest

Come back to what's alive in you

Bufo (5-MeO-DMT) is short and complete. In a few minutes it can quiet the noise and return you to a felt sense of aliveness, the very thing burnout slowly takes away.

Held in a small, private setting with real support, it can help you feel yourself again, and remember there is far more to you than what you produce. It isn't a fix or a treatment. It's a way to reset from the inside.
