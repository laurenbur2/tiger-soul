1. The nature of the medicine

Bufo alvarius, the Sonoran Desert Toad, carries a secretion containing 5-MeO-DMT, a naturally occurring compound used for centuries in indigenous ceremony. It is received by smoking, which brings a rapid onset, and the journey itself is short, usually 15 to 30 minutes. Many describe an intense, non-ordinary state of consciousness that can include a dissolution of the ordinary sense of self.

The Bufo medicine is ethically sourced from the Sonoran Desert through responsible practices that prioritize the well-being of the toads and environmental conservation. It is obtained through trusted relationships with experienced practitioners, and is handled, stored, and administered with the highest standards of care and reverence.

2. Potential benefits

People come to this medicine for different reasons, and no two journeys are alike. Participants often describe a profound sense of unity and clarity, the release of emotional and mental weight carried for years, and a deep calm in the days that follow. These are spiritual and personal experiences, not medical outcomes. We make no promise or guarantee of any particular result, healing, or benefit.

3. Risks, and what to expect

5-MeO-DMT is powerful, and the experience can be physically and emotionally intense while it lasts. You should understand and accept the following before taking part:

  • Strong physical effects are common, including a raised heart rate, changes in blood pressure, physical purging such as nausea or vomiting, shaking, and temporary loss of physical control.
  • Overwhelming emotional and psychological states can arise, including fear, confusion, disorientation, the surfacing of difficult memories or trauma, and the dissolution of the ordinary sense of self.
  • It is not safe alongside certain medications, including but not limited to SSRIs, MAOIs, and some other psychiatric medications, or certain heart, blood-pressure, neurological, and mental-health conditions. This is why the health and medical screening is required, and why it must be complete and honest.
  • As with any non-ordinary state, there are risks that cannot be fully predicted or listed here. You accept that no screening or preparation can remove all risk.

6. Physical and psychological safety

We reduce risk through the way we hold ceremony, at every stage:

  • Everyone completes a full medical and psychological screening that we read personally before anything begins, and anyone with a contraindicated condition, vulnerability, or medication is not cleared to join.
  • We follow up with a one-to-one video call, and prepare each person in advance.
  • We give careful attention to set, setting, and space preparation.
  • During ceremony your facilitator remains present with you the entire time, in a private, protected space, with small numbers and a harm-reduction approach.
  • We ask you to consult your physician beforehand, and we will tell you honestly if we do not believe it is safe for you to take part.
