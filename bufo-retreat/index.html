A Bufo retreat, held the way it should be

What a Tiger Soul Retreat Is

A Bufo retreat with Tiger Soul is a small, private, deeply held container in the jungle and along the coast of Mexico. Everything, the screening, the way ceremony is held, the nature around you, and the support long after, is arranged so you can do the real work safely.

In ceremony

How Ceremony Is Held

Nothing left to chance

Bufo (5-MeO-DMT) is short and total, often only minutes, but those minutes ask everything of you. So we hold them with great care. You are never alone. A trained facilitator is with you one to one through the entire ceremony, from the first breath to the return.

Each ceremony follows one gentle arc, arrival, surrender, and return, supported by rapé and sound to help you settle before and land after. There is no rushing, no crowd, and no pressure. Only a safe, held space to meet the medicine and come home to yourself.

Safety First, Always

The safety is handled before you ever sit

Bufo commands respect, and most of the work of a safe ceremony happens long before ceremony day. This is what is always in place.

  • Medical and mental-health screening before you're accepted, so no one sits who shouldn't
  • A one-to-one preparation call with your facilitator, and a full preparation guide
  • Trained facilitators with you one to one through every ceremony
  • A small group, never a crowd, in a private space that stays yours
  • Rapé, sound, and experienced hands to help you settle and land
  • A full month of integration support after you fly home

In the jungle

Held by Nature

The land does its own work

We hold our retreats in the jungle and along the coast of Mexico for a reason. Nature is part of the medicine. Between ceremonies there is time to rest by the pool, swim in a cenote, and let the quiet of the jungle settle your nervous system.

Mornings open gently with movement, breathwork, and sound. The days are not packed, they are spacious, so the land, the water, and the stillness can do their own quiet work alongside the medicine.

Riviera Maya, Mexico

Where You'll Be

Tulum and Puerto Morelos, Mexico

Our Bufo retreats are held in private villas in the Riviera Maya, the jungle and turquoise water of Tulum, and the quiet jungle of Puerto Morelos. Small, private, and yours for the length of the retreat.

No lobbies, no strangers, no need to leave the container to find a quiet corner. Just a safe home base surrounded by the nature that makes this work possible.

Before & After

Held long before, and long after

The retreat is the middle of the journey, not the whole of it. You are prepared for weeks before you arrive, and supported for a month after you leave. All of it is included.

I The weeks before

Preparation & education

You won't arrive cold. Everyone is screened, prepared, and taught what they're walking into, so the first day isn't the first time you've thought about any of it.

  • Health screening and intake review
  • A one-to-one call with your facilitator
  • A preparation guide: food, substances, medication, rest
  • Education on Bufo, and how safety is held
  • A group welcome call before you arrive
II The month after

Integration, together

What happens after the retreat is where the change is kept or lost, so we stay with you, as a group and one to one, for a full month.

  • A personal check-in soon after the retreat
  • A group integration call about a week later
  • A second group call at twenty-eight days
  • A personal thirty-day commitment plan
  • Ongoing connection with the Tiger Soul community
