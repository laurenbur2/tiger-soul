A Mind That Never Clocks Out

Anxiety is exhausting precisely because it never clocks out. The mind races, the body braces, and even rest feels like waiting for the next thing to worry about. You can know your fears aren't rational and still not be able to stop them.

The Loop That Won't Stop

A mind that won't let go

Chronic anxiety is a loop, thoughts feeding feelings feeding thoughts, with no off switch. Coping tools help you manage it, but managing isn't the same as being free of it.

Underneath the noise is a nervous system that never quite learned it was safe to rest. That's the layer most approaches never reach.

Medicine of the Gods

A Return to Stillness

Remember what calm feels like

Bufo (5-MeO-DMT) can dissolve the mental noise in minutes and return you to a deep, felt stillness, the ground anxiety keeps you from. Many people describe it as the quietest their mind has ever been.

Held safely, with support before and after, that stillness can become something you carry home, not just a moment on the mat. It isn't a fix or a treatment for anxiety. It's a way to remember your nervous system knows how to rest.
