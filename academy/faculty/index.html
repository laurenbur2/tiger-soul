Standards

What we hold ourselves to.

There is no licensing body for this field. In the absence of one, the least we can do is publish our own standard and let people hold us to it.

01

We say when we do not know

The evidence base here is small. Where the literature is thin or contested, we say so in class and in the library rather than rounding it up to certainty.

02

Screening is separate from sales

The person assessing whether you should be here is not the person whose income depends on your answer.

03

The library stays free

No email gate, no funnel. Information that keeps people safe should not sit behind a purchase.

04

Students can fail

An assessment that nobody fails is a receipt. Ours is not.

05

We do not claim what we are not

The Academy is an education provider. It is not a licensing body, not a medical institution, and not a regulator. Our certificates say exactly that.
