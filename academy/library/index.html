The Library

Open, free,
and kept honest.

Twelve subjects on ethical preparation and integration. No email wall. Where the evidence is thin we say so, and where we disagree with the popular account we say that too.

This library is educational. It is not medical advice, it is not a clinical protocol, and it does not tell anyone how to use any substance. If you are making a decision about your health, talk to a doctor who knows your history.
