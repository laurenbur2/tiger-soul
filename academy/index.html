What this is

A school, not a marketing page.

Most of what circulates about 5-MeO-DMT online is either breathless or frightened. Very little of it is careful. The Academy exists to close that gap.

We teach what is actually known: the receptor pharmacology, the physiology, the contraindications, the conservation questions around the Sonoran Desert toad, the ethics of consent, and the slow work of integration in the months that follow. Where the evidence is thin, we say so.
