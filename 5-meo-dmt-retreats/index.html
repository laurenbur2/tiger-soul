Questions

Good to know

What is 5-MeO-DMT, and how is the medicine obtained?

5-MeO-DMT is a naturally occurring compound carried in the secretion of the Bufo alvarius toad — the Sonoran Desert Toad — used for centuries in indigenous ceremony. The medicine is harvested sustainably and respectfully, and is received by smoking, which brings a rapid onset. The journey itself is short, usually 15 to 30 minutes. For the full picture, read what Bufo and 5-MeO-DMT are.

What are the benefits?

People come to Bufo for different reasons, and no two journeys are alike. Many describe a profound sense of unity and clarity, the release of emotional and mental weight they've carried for years, and a deep calm in the days that follow. It's often met as a spiritual homecoming — a chance to set down old patterns and reconnect with who you are underneath them. These are spiritual and personal experiences, not medical outcomes, and we never promise a particular result.

What are the risks?

5-MeO-DMT is powerful, and we treat it that way. It isn't safe alongside certain medications or heart, blood-pressure, and mental-health conditions — which is exactly why the health screening comes first, and why it's thorough. The experience can be physically and emotionally intense while it lasts. Most risk comes from working outside a safe, screened, well-held setting; we reduce it with careful screening, an experienced facilitator present the whole time, and a calm, controlled space. Tiger Soul offerings are a spiritual practice, not medical treatment — please consult your physician before any ceremony.

What safety measures do you have in place?

Several, and none are optional. Everyone completes a full health and medical screening that we read personally, so we know your history, medications, and conditions before anything begins. We follow up with a one-to-one conversation. Ceremony is held by an experienced facilitator who stays with you throughout, in a private, protected space — never a crowd, never rushed. We also ask you to consult your physician beforehand. If something isn't safe for you, we'll tell you honestly.

What's the process to be approved?

Every journey begins with our health and medical intake screening — required for every offering. The facilitators read it personally, then arrange a call to answer your questions and make sure this is your time. Before ceremony you'll also complete a consent and waiver form. Once you're cleared and everything's in place, we hold your spot and send your preparation and retreat details.

Is there preparation beforehand?

Yes — you won't arrive cold. You're prepared for weeks before you travel: screened, taught what you're walking into, and guided on how to ready your body and mind. For the Practitioner Training, a study manual arrives about 30 days ahead (around 20 hours of material). By the time you arrive, none of it is unfamiliar.

Is there integration and support afterward?

Always. The days after a ceremony are where the work settles, so we stay with you — as a group and one to one. Retreat guests are supported for about a month after returning home; practitioner trainees continue with weekly gatherings and a private group of fellow facilitators. Integration is included, not an add-on.
